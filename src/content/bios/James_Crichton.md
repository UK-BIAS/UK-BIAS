James is Deputy Director of Bioimaging at the University of Exeter and leads Imaging and Image Analysis at the RILD building, supporting a broad range of translational biomedical projects. His background in reproductive biology sparked a long-standing love of microscopy, spanning super-resolution imaging, high-throughput screening, and digital pathology. He is passionate about developing scalable, reproducible analysis workflows using open-source tools and helping researchers extract meaningful insights from complex imaging datasets. Outside the lab he enjoys staying active and exploring.
