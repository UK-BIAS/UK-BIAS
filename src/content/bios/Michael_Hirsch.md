Michael is a Senior Facility Scientist at the Octopus imaging facility within STFC's Central Laser Facility at the Rutherford Appleton Laboratory in Oxfordshire. With over 15 years of experience in image analysis, he brings a strong background in Mathematics and a PhD in Theoretical Chemistry. His expertise spans single-molecule image analysis and tracking, spatial analysis, segmentation, and photophysics under cryogenic conditions. Michael has worked with data from a diverse range of instruments including TIRF microscopy, STORM, SEM, and light-sheet microscopes, and has also conducted imaging camera characterisation. He regularly works with compute clusters, software containers, and large datasets, and is a strong advocate for FAIR data management principles.
